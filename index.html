About Kinh Bac
Wood?

Our company manufactures and exports high-quality wood products in Vietnam. We maintain strict control over our raw material sourcing and ensure a stable supply to meet the needs of our customers worldwide.

With our production capacity and partnerships with over 50 leading wood manufacturers in Vietnam, we operate dedicated production facilities that allow for rigorous control over raw materials and product quality. OUR STORY

About the Nature of Kinh Bac Wood...

Based in Vietnam, our company is a leading exporter of premium-quality wood products Vietnam, trusted by partners across USA, Asia, the Middle East, and Europe. What sets us apart is our deep commitment to quality from the very foundation — the raw materials and production process.

We work closely with reliable wood sourcing areas in Vietnam’s key timber regions, ensuring stable and legal raw material supply. Our manufacturing partners operate large-scale, modern facilities that follow strict production protocols. From veneer selection to pressing and finishing, every stage is carefully controlled. We strictly supervise adhesive usage, moisture content, and technical specifications to ensure our wood products meets international standards such as CARB, FSC, BIS and other market-specific requirements.

Our vertically integrated supply chain allows us to maintain consistency, traceability, and product reliability from factory to port. We collaborate with experienced engineers, QC teams, and local suppliers to ensure sustainable sourcing while meeting the growing global demand for high-quality wood products.

With a strong focus on quality assurance and transparency from production to export, we are proud to deliver Vietnamese wood products that meet the highest expectations of international markets.
