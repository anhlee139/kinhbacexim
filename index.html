About Kinh Bac
Plywood?

With over 10 years in this field, I've had the chance to travel, meet many customers, and hear one common concern: How to find a reputable, trustworthy plywood manufacturer and supplier.

Aware of that, I decided to establish Kinh Bac International Import - Export Company Limited (KINH BAC PLYWOOD), with the goal of promoting Vietnamese plywood products and culture to international friends.

With our partnerships with over 50 leading plywood manufacturers in Vietnam, we operate dedicated production facilities that allow for rigorous control over raw materials, product quality and ensure a stable supply to meet the needs of our customers worldwide.

Our experienced Quality Control (QC) team is capable of meeting even the most stringent international standards. We also support flexible partnerships by accepting small initial orders and offering competitive pricing to facilitate starting a business with us.

To me, a product is more than a tradable good — it reflects our aspirations and Vietnamese cultural values.

I look forward to future opportunities to cooperate with friends around the world, building the KINH BAC PLYWOOD brand — a Vietnamese brand unlike any other.

KINH BAC PLYWOOD has been, and always will be, with you.

OUR STORY

About the Nature of Kinh Bac Plywood...

Based in Vietnam, our company is a leading exporter of premium-quality plywood products Vietnam, trusted by partners across USA, Asia, the Middle East, and Europe. What sets us apart is our deep commitment to quality from the very foundation — the raw materials and production process.

We work closely with reliable wood sourcing areas in Vietnam's key timber regions, ensuring stable and legal raw material supply. Our manufacturing partners operate large-scale, modern facilities that follow strict production protocols. From veneer selection to pressing and finishing, every stage is carefully controlled. We strictly supervise glue usage, moisture content, and technical specifications to ensure our Plywood products meets international standards such as E0, E1, E2, CARB P2, FSC, CE, and other market-specific requirements.

Our vertically integrated supply chain allows us to maintain consistency, traceability, and product reliability from factory to port. We collaborate with experienced engineers, QC teams, and local suppliers to ensure sustainable sourcing while meeting the growing global demand for high-quality Plywood products.

With a strong focus on quality assurance and transparency from production to export, we are proud to deliver Vietnamese plywood products that meet the highest expectations of international markets.
